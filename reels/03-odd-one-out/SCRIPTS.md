# YouTube Short scripts — family puzzle formats

Who these are for: everyone at once — kids, parents and grandparents. All four formats work **with the sound off**, use **no voiceover** (synthetic voices are the fastest "this is AI" tell, and text works in every language), and are made from code, emoji graphics and synthesized sound, so nothing is AI-generated footage pretending to be real.

Why these formats get views: Shorts are ranked largely on **viewed vs. swiped away**, **average % watched** (over 100 % means people rewatch) and **comments/shares**. Puzzles and "pick one" videos push all three: people stop to play, rewind to check, and comment their score.

## ★ Script 1 — "Find the Odd One Out: 3 Levels" (produced, see `build/`)

**Length:** ~25 s · **Hook:** the puzzle is already on screen on frame 0 with a ticking timer — the viewer is playing before they decide to scroll.

| Time | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Title **FIND THE ODD ONE OUT 👀**, pill **LEVEL 1/3: EASY** (green). Grid of 48 🍎, one is a 🍅. Timer bar starts draining, big **4**. | Tick every second over a bouncy pizzicato loop |
| 0–4 s | Timer 4 → 1, bar green → yellow → red, number pulses on the last two seconds | Ticks get higher on 2 and 1 |
| 4.0 | Everything else dims, the 🍅 pops up with a red ring drawn around it, confetti, **FOUND IT? ✅** | Ding + sparkle |
| ~5.7 | Cells shrink away, new grid pops in as a wave: 48 😃, one 😄. Pill **LEVEL 2/3: MEDIUM** (orange). 5 s timer. | Whoosh, bubble pops |
| ~12 | Reveal 😄 | Ding |
| ~13.7 | **LEVEL 3/3: HARD** (red). 48 🐔, one is **facing the other way**. 6 s timer. | Whoosh, pops |
| ~21 | Reveal: **WOW! 🔥 YOU'RE A GENIUS IF YOU GOT IT** | Bigger fanfare |
| ~22.8 | End card: **HOW MANY DID YOU FIND?** with three bubbles **1 · 2 · 3** and **COMMENT BELOW 👇**, **SUBSCRIBE** | Ta-da |

**Title:** `Find the odd one out 👀 Can you get all 3? #puzzle #shorts`
**Description:** `Level 3 is tricky! Comment how many you found 👇`
**Why it works:** instant interaction, difficulty ramp keeps people for level 3, the comment prompt has a one-character answer (easy for kids and grandparents), and people rewind to find the ones they missed (rewatches).
**Series potential:** endless — swap emoji pairs, grid size, time limits. Each episode is genuinely different puzzles, which matters for YouTube's "repetitive content" monetization rule.

## Script 2 — "Guess the Animal by Its Sound 🔊"

**Length:** ~30 s, 4 animals · **Hook (frame 0):** a black silhouette with **WHO'S MAKING THIS SOUND? 🔊** and the sound already playing.

| Beat | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Each animal (7 s) | Black silhouette of the emoji wobbles, 3 answer bubbles (e.g. 🐸 / 🦆 / 🐷), 3-2-1 | Real animal recording (CC0, e.g. BigSoundBank), 3 ticks |
| Reveal | Silhouette fills with colour, correct bubble turns green, ✅ | Ding |
| Order | Easy (cow) → medium (duck) → hard (owl) → bonus (dolphin) | |
| End | **HOW MANY DID YOU GET? 👇** | Ta-da |

**Why it works:** sound-on hook, educational (parents and teachers share it), multiple choice means even small kids can play.

## Script 3 — "Pick a Colour Before It Starts! 🏁" (marble race)

**Length:** ~25 s · **Hook:** four marbles at the top of a track, **PICK ONE — QUICK!** and a 3-2-1.

| Beat | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | 🔴🔵🟢🟡 marbles, countdown | Drum roll |
| 2–20 s | Physics race down zig-zag ramps, a spinner, a trap door; leader changes 3 times; a "commentary" banner (**BLUE TAKES THE LEAD!**) | Rolling, bonks, crowd "ooh" synth |
| 20 s | Photo finish, slow-mo, winner podium | Fanfare |
| End | **DID YOUR COLOUR WIN? 👇** | |

**Why it works:** everyone has a stake within one second; outcome uncertainty holds attention to the end; 4-way split gives comment arguments.

## YouTube policy checklist (apply to every upload)

- **Altered or synthetic content label:** required only for *realistic* content that viewers could mistake for real people, places or events. These cartoon/emoji puzzles aren't realistic and aren't AI-generated, so no label is needed. If you ever post realistic AI footage, tick "Altered content: Yes" — not doing so can get videos removed or the channel penalized.
- **Made for kids:** you must answer honestly. If young children are the *primary* audience, set **Made for kids** (comments are then off, ads are non-personalized). Puzzles aimed at a general family audience can be "Not made for kids" — but if you choose that, keep titles and thumbnails family-general (not "for toddlers").
- **No misleading metadata:** the title must match the video. "Can you get all 3?" is fine; fake claims ("only 1 % can solve this", "real footage") are a risk.
- **Repetitive / mass-produced content (YPP monetization):** each episode needs new puzzles, not the same video with tiny changes. Keep a library of different pairs and formats.
- **Copyright:** all assets here are licensed for commercial use — Fluent Emoji (MIT), Luckiest Guy font (Apache 2.0), CC0 sounds, original synthesized music. Don't add copyrighted music outside the Shorts audio library.
- **Engagement bait:** asking people to comment their score is fine. Don't offer fake rewards ("comment to win a phone") or say "like or bad luck".
